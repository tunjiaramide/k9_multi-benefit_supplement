Buying a dog supplement in Lagos isn't just about finding the right ingredients. You also need to consider product authenticity, storage, delivery and whether you'll be able to buy the same product again when you need it.

Here are a few practical things to check before placing your order.

## Buy From a Reliable Seller

Dog supplements should come with clear product information and identifiable sellers. Before paying, check that the seller provides:

- A genuine business or verified selling page.
- Clear ingredient information and serving instructions.
- Contact details and a process for handling complaints.
- Transparent pricing and delivery information.

Be cautious of listings that provide little information about the product or seller.

## Pay Attention to Storage

Lagos's warm, humid climate makes storage worth considering, especially for products containing oils or live probiotic cultures.

When your supplement arrives:

- Check the expiry date and packaging condition.
- Store it according to the manufacturer's instructions.
- Keep it away from direct sunlight and excessive heat.
- Avoid products with damaged packaging or signs of improper storage.

A product's label may look reassuring, but how it's handled before reaching you matters too.

## Check Delivery and Restocking

Delivery options can vary depending on whether you're on the Island, Mainland or farther out of the city.

Before ordering, confirm the expected delivery window, delivery fees and how you'll be notified if your order is delayed. If your dog takes a supplement regularly, check whether the seller stocks it consistently.

It's also sensible to reorder before the container runs out rather than waiting until the last day.

## Is It Better to Buy Online or In-Store?

Both options have advantages.

**Physical pet shops** allow you to inspect the packaging and expiry date before purchasing. However, stock and product selection may vary.

**Online retailers** offer convenience and can make comparing products easier, but you depend on the seller to store and transport the product appropriately.

Whichever route you choose, check the same things: authenticity, product details, expiry date, storage and seller reliability.

## Choose the Supplement for Your Dog's Needs

A joint supplement, probiotic or vitamin formula won't serve every purpose equally well. Start by identifying what you're trying to support, then compare the ingredients and amounts in products designed for that purpose.

If your dog has ongoing symptoms or an existing health condition, consult your veterinarian before introducing a supplement.
