A dull coat or dry, flaky skin can make you wonder whether your dog is getting the right nutrition. Salmon oil is one ingredient commonly found in dog supplements designed to support healthy skin and coat, but there's more to consider than the name on the bottle.

## What Makes Salmon Oil Useful?

Salmon oil provides omega-3 fatty acids, including EPA and DHA, which play important roles in normal bodily functions.

These fatty acids are commonly included in dog supplements to support skin and coat health. The actual nutritional contribution depends on the oil's composition and the amount provided per serving.

## Does the Source Matter?

You'll come across products labelled "wild Alaskan salmon oil," "fish oil" or simply "omega-3 oil."

A specific sourcing claim tells you more about where an ingredient comes from than a generic description. However, the source alone doesn't guarantee quality. Look for clear product information, appropriate quality controls and details about the fatty acids provided where available.

## Beyond a Shiny Coat

Omega-3 fatty acids contribute to normal bodily functions beyond skin and coat maintenance. However, adding salmon oil doesn't automatically mean your dog needs more omega-3, especially if their existing diet already provides appropriate amounts.

It's also important to remember that skin problems and excessive shedding aren't always nutritional issues. Parasites, allergies, infections and other conditions can affect your dog's coat and skin.

## What Should You Check Before Buying?

Keep these four things in mind:

- **Amount per serving:** Look for the quantity of salmon oil and, where available, the EPA and DHA content.
- **Storage:** Oils can deteriorate when exposed to heat, light and air. Follow the product's storage instructions.
- **Product quality:** Choose a product with clear ingredient information and reliable sourcing.
- **Format:** Salmon oil comes in liquids, capsules and soft chews. Choose one that suits your dog's routine without overlooking the actual amount provided.

Nigeria's warm climate makes proper storage particularly important. Don't leave supplements in a hot car or in direct sunlight, and check the expiry date before use.

## Salmon Oil in a Liquid or a Soft Chew?

Liquid salmon oil can be added to food and may allow flexible serving adjustments. Capsules offer a separate, measured format, while soft chews can make it easier to include salmon oil alongside other ingredients.

The best format depends on your dog and how easily you can give it consistently. Whichever you choose, compare the actual nutritional content rather than assuming all products provide the same amount.
